# Scientific evidence and status

## Reading these claims

The figures below are developer-reported outcomes from controlled simulations and project records. They are not peer-reviewed findings, and no independent external audit has been completed. Hidden simulator truth is used by the developer for post-run evaluation only; it should not be available to the scientist during a correctly sealed run.

## Primary prototype

The primary prototype has a controlled mechanics research loop: noisy measurements, competing quantitative hypotheses, model comparison, derived predictions, further experiments, anomaly/residual inspection, claim revision, and persistent research records. Its available model grammar and action space are bounded. Certain regimes, especially strongly damped pendulum cases, remain difficult.

## Frontier development evidence

In a developer-authored development world, a recorded run completed 275 experiments in about 7.5 seconds. The scientist formed a material-inertia concept from cross-material acceleration ratios; a post-run developer comparison reported inferred ratios within roughly 1–2% of sealed values. It also found two interaction channels and explored latent cyclic-phase and continuous-direction representations. These results arose in a world authored during development, so they do not establish performance on independently designed worlds.

## Held-out evaluation and failures

A separate held-out run scored 3.61, below the existing supported/acceptance threshold. Missed effects included field build-up, velocity-dependent push, and screening; some tests were underpowered for particular mechanisms. The acceptance bar was not changed to make the result pass. This is important negative evidence and a reason to improve representation growth and experiment design.

## Claims we do not make

- We do not claim the current system has discovered new real-world physics.
- We do not claim open-ended hypothesis invention.
- We do not claim Frontier reliably generalizes beyond its development language and worlds.
- We do not claim independent peer review or external reproduction.
- We do not conflate simulated observations, developer truth checks, and the scientist's own beliefs.

## What would strengthen the evidence

A future release should include a versioned source revision, dependency configuration, exact run commands, public development seeds, immutable ledgers, the predeclared scoring rule, a post-run truth-comparison report, and sufficient environment information to reproduce measured results. Sealed evaluation truths should be published only after the evaluation protocol permits unsealing.

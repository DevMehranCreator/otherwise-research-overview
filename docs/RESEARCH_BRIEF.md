# Otherwise — research brief

**Maintainer:** Mehran, independent developer  
**Contact:** otherwise532@gmail.com  
**Status:** early-stage research prototype

## Question

Can a computational scientist infer hidden structure from noisy experiments, make predictions on new observations, identify when its present hypothesis family is inadequate, and revise claims without relying on a language model to decide scientific truth?

## Approach

Otherwise studies this question in controlled synthetic mechanics. Its research loop involves noisy observations, quantitative hypothesis comparison, prediction derivation, discriminating experiment selection, residual analysis, and versioned claim revision. The private scientific engine and canonical memory are not included in this public overview.

## Preliminary evidence

In a developer-authored Frontier development world, one recorded run completed 275 experiments in approximately 7.5 seconds. Developer post-run comparison reported material-inertia ratios within roughly 1–2% of sealed values; model comparison also favored two interaction channels over a simpler single-channel/material-charge explanation. These results are preliminary and not independently audited.

## Known limitation

A separate held-out evaluation scored 3.61, below the existing acceptance threshold, and missed field build-up, velocity-dependent interaction, and screening. The threshold was not changed to make the result pass. The representation grammar remains limited, and Frontier is not fully integrated with the primary prototype.

## Proposed work

Freeze the evaluation protocol and threshold; test across reproducible seeds; separate development and held-out worlds; measure prediction quality, uncertainty calibration, failure detection, representation limitations, and compute cost; report failed and inconclusive runs.

## Claims boundary

No validated real-world physical discovery, peer review, independent reproduction, or open-ended scientific intelligence is claimed. This brief describes a proposal and preliminary controlled-simulation evidence, not a completed external study.

## Requested feedback

Technical critique of the evaluation method, suitable benchmarks, reproducibility advice, and possible research collaboration.

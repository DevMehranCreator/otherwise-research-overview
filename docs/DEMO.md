The recorded prototype shows the primary Otherwise interface, including its scientific-world view, ongoing experiment activity, control-room/scientist panels, and persistent research record.

## Important scope note

This recording represents the **primary Otherwise prototype**. The experimental Frontier branch is separate and is not integrated into this 3D interface. The recording is a product demonstration, not an independent replication or peer review of the scientific results.

## Sharing check

The Google Drive file must be accessible to intended reviewers. In Drive, open **Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer** if you want readers who are not signed in to your Google account to watch it. Do not grant editor access for a public demo.

This repository links to the hosted recording rather than storing the source video here. The source recording is large, and the project repository is intended to remain a lightweight research overview.

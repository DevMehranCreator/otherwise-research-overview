# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Otherwise.

This repository currently contains public research documentation rather than the private engine source. Useful contributions include carefully sourced technical feedback, suggestions for evaluation metrics, reproducibility checklists, and references to relevant scientific-agent benchmarks.

## Research integrity

- Distinguish measured results, developer interpretation, and future plans.
- Keep negative and inconclusive results visible.
- Do not describe simulated outcomes as validated real-world discoveries.
- Do not alter a frozen acceptance criterion after seeing results.
- Do not upload private source code, credentials, private memory, or sealed evaluation materials.

For substantial proposed edits, open an issue describing the question, evidence, and intended change.
